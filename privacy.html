Privacy Policy for Idemo

Effective date: July 1, 2026

Idemo ("Idemo", "we", "us") is a mobile app for learning the Bosnian language, operated by AJ Digital Services LLC. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using Idemo you agree to this policy.

Contact: questions or requests — support@ajdigitalservices.ai.

Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). You can also use the app as a guest (an anonymous account is created automatically so your progress can be saved); a guest account has no email or personal identifier.

Learning data. To run the course and remember your progress, we store data tied to your account, such as: modules completed, lesson and review attempts, spaced-repetition review state, and your chosen interface language. This is used only to operate the app for you.

Purchase information. If you subscribe, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google (and our subscription manager, RevenueCat). We receive a record that you have an active subscription. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

Device and technical data. Standard information needed to run a mobile app, such as app version and basic error/diagnostic information, plus data stored locally on your device (your session, language preference, and sound setting).

We do NOT collect: location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. Idemo contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To provide the course and save your progress across devices.
  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide audio pronunciation (see "Audio / text-to-speech" below).
  • To process and restore subscriptions.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the app working and secure.

Audio / text-to-speech

Spoken audio for lessons is generated by ElevenLabs from the app's own curriculum text (the Bosnian phrases in the course). The text sent for audio generation is course content, not your personal information. Generated audio is cached so it is not re-generated on every use.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us operate the app, under their own terms and security:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech audio generation (course text only).
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and subscription billing.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status management.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and learning data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, which permanently removes your account and all associated learning data. You may also request deletion by emailing support@ajdigitalservices.ai.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, use in-app account deletion or contact support@ajdigitalservices.ai. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

Idemo is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, row-level database access controls so users can only access their own data). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

International transfers

Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country (including the United States and the EU). By using Idemo you consent to such transfers, handled under appropriate safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
