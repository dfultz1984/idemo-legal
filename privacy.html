Privacy Policy for Idemo

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Idemo ("Idemo", "we", "us", "our") is a mobile app for learning the Bosnian language. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, how we use and protect it, and the rights and choices you have. By using Idemo you agree to this policy.

Who we are (data controller)

The controller responsible for your personal information is AJ Digital Services LLC (Florida, United States). For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@ajdigitalservices.ai.

Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase). You can also use the app as a guest. An anonymous account with a randomly generated account ID is created automatically so your progress can be saved; it has no email, name, or other information you type in.

Learning data. To run the course and remember your progress, we store data tied to your account, such as: modules completed, lesson and review attempts, spaced-repetition review state, and your chosen interface language. This is used only to operate the app for you.

Purchase information. If you subscribe, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google and managed for us by RevenueCat. We and RevenueCat receive the product, transaction, subscription, purchase-history, and entitlement information needed to provide and restore Premium access. We never receive or store your card or payment details.

Device and technical data. Standard information needed to run a mobile app, such as app version and basic error/diagnostic information, plus data stored locally on your device (your session, language preference, and sound setting). Idemo uses local device storage, not web browser cookies.

We do NOT collect: precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers. Idemo contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect "sensitive personal information" as defined under California law.

Legal bases for processing (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal information under the following legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR):

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account and provide the course you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep Idemo secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain and improve the app's reliability, in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Consent — where we specifically ask for it; you may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process information to comply with applicable law.

Audio / text-to-speech

Spoken audio for lessons is generated by ElevenLabs from the app's own curriculum text (the Bosnian phrases in the course). The text sent for audio generation is course content, not your personal information. Generated audio is cached so it is not re-generated on every use.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us operate the app, under their own terms and security:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech audio generation (course text only).
  • Apple / Google — app distribution and subscription billing.
  • RevenueCat — purchase, subscription, and entitlement management.
  • Sentry — limited errors-only crash and diagnostic reporting.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How to delete your account and your data

You can delete your account and all associated learning data at any time, in two ways:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated learning data.
  • By request: email support@ajdigitalservices.ai from the address on your account and we will delete your account and data.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our systems and instruct our processors to do the same. We may retain a limited amount of information where necessary for security, fraud prevention, or to comply with a legal obligation, and only for as long as needed for that purpose.

Data retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account exists, so we can provide the service and remember your progress. When you delete your account (see above), we delete that data, subject to the limited exceptions described there.

Technical logs and deletion records

Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) automatically keeps standard technical request logs — such as IP address, browser information, and an account identifier — for up to 7 days. These logs exist for security and operational reasons only; we do not use them for analytics, profiling, or marketing, and they delete automatically.

When you delete your account, all of your personal data and learning progress are permanently removed. To make sure a deleted account stays deleted, we keep one pseudonymous deletion record: a scrambled code (derived from your account ID using a one-way cryptographic function) that cannot be turned back into your identity or linked to you by anyone without access to our private keys. This record exists solely so that if we ever restore data from a backup, your deleted account is not accidentally brought back. It is stored with Google Cloud, which acts as our processor for this single purpose.

Where we transfer personal information across borders, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent mechanism.
